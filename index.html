The evening

A proper night out for a proper cause.

Hosted by Robert Drury, Chairman of Lincoln Business Club, this is a friendly quiz night with all the trimmings. Sharp rounds, a proper bar, and a buffet-style curry to keep teams fuelled between rounds.

Teams are six strong. Bring colleagues, neighbours, the family, or cobble together a group from your local. Whoever you rally, every ticket sold goes towards Alzheimer's fundraising in our community.

Guardian Angel Carers Lincoln & Newark see the impact of dementia every day. Together with Lincolnshire Marketing, we're putting on a brilliant evening to do something about it.
